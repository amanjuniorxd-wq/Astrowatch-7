## Caveats

- Sample is curated from general biographical knowledge, not individually re-verified live at this volume (a couple of very recent honorees were spot-checked live this session).
- 447 of 459 (97%) use ASSUMED_NOON -- Ascendant/house results are only meaningful for the small DOCUMENTED subset.
- No held-out set, no significance testing, no multiple-comparison correction -- consistent with every other exploratory report in this project. With 7 fields x a dozen+ Rasi/lord values, many cells will look 'notable' by chance alone at this sample size.
- Fields are unevenly sampled (Athletes and Scientists are larger than Artists/Directors, for instance) -- frequency differences may just reflect sample size, not any real astrological effect.
